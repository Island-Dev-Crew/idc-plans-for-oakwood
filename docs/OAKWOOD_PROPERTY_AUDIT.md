## What was already unusually strong

1. **Room-grounded planning.** The May 26 walkthrough moves the idea beyond a mood board.
2. **Funding firewall.** Equipment, facility labor and sustainment are separated.
3. **Multi-modal lab model.** Local compute, edge/physical AI, media, fabrication and instruction coexist.
4. **Student initiative is preserved.** The 12-student Campus Companion team is treated as a foundation, not a failure.
5. **Concrete production evidence.** Enhanced Campus Companion records 51 buildings, 57 departments, 94 accessibility attributes and 27 iterations in IDC project evidence.
6. **Responsible-AI intent.** Ethics and governance were present before they became fashionable.

## Material gaps in the previous ecosystem

| Gap | Consequence | 2× correction |

| Some 2025 AI references were tool-specific | rapid staleness | August 2026 capability map: agentic, local/open, multimodal, physical AI, evaluation/governance |

## Dr. Smith concern crosswalk

This crosswalk is based on the **concerns documented in the Campus Companion project record**, not a claim that every item below is a verbatim quotation from Dr. Shushannah Smith.

| Documented concern | Enhanced Campus Companion response | New institutional rule |
|---|---|---|

## Campus Companion as validator—not replacement

The application should become the first **Proof Ledger reference case**:

- honor the original 12-student team led by Ramy Campusano;
- show baseline, enhancement and decisions without humiliation language;
- publish what was built, tested, improved and learned;
- separate student contributions from IDC intervention;
- issue a LinkedIn-friendly public credential page only when underlying evidence is inspectable;
- avoid claiming LinkedIn offers an unrestricted custom candidate-scoring API.

Remaining uncertainty concerns unpublished committee decisions, classroom outcomes, procurement approval, precise room measurements and any private AAMU or Oakwood plans not present in the reviewed sources.
