# Iron Canvas v5.2 — MISSION Design PRD

## Mission
Build **IDC Plans for Oakwood — The AI Production & Proof Institute**, a premium, evidence-aware compilation hub that unifies Jon Isaac’s Oakwood AI work, compares it fairly with AAMU’s public 2026 program, and proposes an actionable 2× model.

## Design direction: “Heritage Signal / Research Ledger”

Not a futuristic AI template. The experience should feel like an archival university prospectus, a systems-control room, and a serious design journal occupying the same page.

### Site DNA
- **Identity:** Oakwood heritage + Huntsville engineering + IDC execution
- **Audience:** Dr. Smith, faculty, administration, grant officers, donors, employers, students
- **Primary action:** understand the decision and open the implementation path
- **Content mode:** evidence, comparison, architecture, curriculum, proof-of-work
- **Emotional arc:** missed moment → clear-eyed benchmark → existing strength → more ambitious future → executable next move

### Feel Profile
- **Editorial:** 9/10
- **Institutional credibility:** 10/10
- **Technical depth:** 8/10
- **Motion:** 6/10 (purposeful, never decorative noise)
- **Heritage warmth:** 7/10
- **Futurism:** 3/10
- **Sales pressure:** 2/10
- **Evidence density:** 9/10

### Treatment register (Claude Design 3)
- **Surface:** deep ink/blue-black canvas, warm ivory reading surfaces, old-gold rules, subtle paper grain
- **Type:** expressive high-contrast serif for thesis lines; precise grotesk/system sans for evidence and controls; monospace for proof labels
- **Composition:** asymmetric editorial grids, oversized score numerals, sticky chapter rail, horizontal lab architecture diagram, no generic 3-card repetition
- **Depth dial:** medium; atmosphere through gradients/grain and photo treatment, restrained shadows, no glassmorphism wall
- **Motion choreography:** thesis reveal, scan-line/progress indicator, scroll-linked chapter state, count-up grades, pipeline nodes, proof-ledger interaction; honor reduced motion
- **Soul artifact:** a living “Proof Signal” line that travels from idea → build → verify → opportunity across the site

## Content architecture
1. **Opening thesis:** “The race was never to announce AI first. It is to turn AI into proof.”
2. **August 2026 reality:** what changed—agentic systems, local/open models, multimodal creation, physical AI, evaluation/governance, model/data provenance.
3. **AAMU benchmark:** exact public program facts and source links; correct the “first HBCU” claim without diminishing its lead.
4. **Confidence board:** AAMU 86 vs current IDC Oakwood 78, with layer-aware explanation and evidence confidence.
5. **What Oakwood already has:** Gamma vision, AI Lab IDC implementation, lab-final strategy, 71-photo site evidence, Campus Companion proof.
6. **2× model:** Foundations + Studio + Proof.
7. **10-seat architecture:** interactive zones and procurement gates.
8. **Curriculum studio:** eight rotating studios and four-year/12-month overlay.
9. **Proof Ledger:** interactive candidate score; anti-gaming caps; LinkedIn/employer portability boundaries.
10. **Campus Companion case:** student initiative + AI mentorship, not student replacement.
11. **Roadmap:** 30/90/180/365-day gates.
12. **Decision room:** three decisions, not a vague “contact us.”
13. **Evidence drawer:** citations, claim labels, source dates, confidence caveats.

## Claim grammar
Every material claim is labeled by type:
- **Verified public fact** — direct external source
- **IDC documented evidence** — repository/photo/build record
- **Planning estimate** — requires quote/validation
- **Proposed target** — ambition, not achieved outcome

## Visual artifacts
- AAMU vs Oakwood radar/score bars (accessible table fallback)
- AAMU 125-credit curriculum strip
- 10-seat floor/zone diagram
- AI workflow constellation: cloud ↔ local ↔ edge
- Proof Ledger scoring instrument
- 30/90/180/365 roadmap
- Verified Oakwood walkthrough/contact imagery
- Existing credible lab renderings with clear “concept rendering” labels

## Accessibility/performance
- Semantic landmarks, skip link, keyboard controls, 44px targets
- Contrast AA minimum
- Reduced motion alternative
- Charts include text/table equivalents
- Lazy-load noncritical images
- No autoplay audio/video
- Standalone portable HTML build plus normal production asset build

## Stop conditions
Do not publish if:
- “first HBCU” is asserted without proof;
- proposal targets are phrased as achieved outcomes;
- AAMU and IDC are compared as though both are degree programs;
- Campus Companion framing belittles the original student team;
- linked credentials are promised as a direct LinkedIn ranking integration;
- room/electrical/procurement estimates are presented as sealed quotes;
- responsive/mobile, reduced-motion, or broken-link QA fails.
